Sticky ginger salmon rice bowl

Feeds 4
4

Ingredients 8 items

  • 600 gSalmon fillet skin off, in chunks
  • 300 gJasmine rice
  • 3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 2 tbspHoney
  • 1 pcsGinger thumb-size, grated
  • 2 pcsCucumber thinly sliced
  • 200 gEdamame beans
  • 2 tspSesame seeds

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with 600 ml water for 12 minutes, then rest with the lid on.
  2. Stir soy, honey and ginger in a bowl. Toss the salmon in half of it.
  3. Fry the salmon 2 minutes per side, pour in the rest and let it turn sticky.
  4. Serve over rice with cucumber and edamame, finish with sesame.
