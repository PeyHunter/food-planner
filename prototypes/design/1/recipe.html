Oven salmon with lemon, dill & new potatoes

35 minAdded by Ronja

Feeds 4 Written for 4 people
4

Ingredients

for 4
  • 600 gsalmon fillet skin on
  • 800 gnew potatoes halved
  • 1 pcslemon zest and juice
  • 20 gfresh dill chopped
  • 200 mlcrème fraîche
  • 1 pcsgarlic clove grated
  • 25 gbutter
  • 2 tbspolive oil at home
  • —salt & pepper at home

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt. Roast for 15 min.

  2. Lay the salmon on the tray, skin down. Brush with the rest of the oil and add a few lemon slices.

  3. Roast for 12–15 min, until the salmon flakes easily.

  4. Stir the crème fraîche with dill, garlic, lemon zest and juice. Season.

  5. Serve with the dill sauce and a knob of butter on the potatoes. For Runi: mash a little salmon and potato with sauce.
