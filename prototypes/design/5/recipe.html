Baked salmon with dill potatoes

Made 3 times · last on 12 Oct by Ronja

feeds 4 Amounts change as you tap
4

Ingredients 8 items

  • 600 gsalmon filletskin on
  • 1 kgnew potatoeshalved
  • 200 ggreen peasfrozen is fine
  • 2 dlcrème fraîche
  • 20 gfresh dillfinely chopped
  • 1 pcslemonzest and juice
  • 2 tbspolive oilalways at home
  • 1 tspflaky saltalways at home

Steps 35 min

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and salt, and roast for 15 minutes.

  2. Lay the salmon on the tray next to the potatoes, skin down. Brush with the rest of the oil and grate over the lemon zest.

  3. Bake 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes but is still a little pink inside.

  4. Meanwhile, boil the peas for 2 minutes. Stir the dill and lemon juice into the crème fraîche.

  5. Serve with the dill sauce on the side. For Runi Lee, mash a small portion with peas and a spoon of sauce.
