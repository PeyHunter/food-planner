Lemony baked salmon with dill potatoes

Fish ❄ freezable kid-friendly

Made 3 times · last on 19 Oct

Ingredients

4people

feeds 4 as written

  • Salmon filletsskin on600 g
  • New potatoeshalved800 g
  • Lemon1 pcs
  • Fresh dillfinely chopped3 tbsp
  • Crème fraîche2 dl
  • Garlicgrated2 pcs
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Salt1 tsp

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Toss the potatoes with half the oil and salt on a tray and roast for 15 minutes.

  2. Mix crème fraîche, garlic, the zest of the lemon and most of the dill.

  3. Push the potatoes aside, lay in the salmon skin down, spoon the dill cream on top and add lemon slices.

  4. Roast another 12–15 minutes until the salmon flakes. Scatter the rest of the dill and serve.
