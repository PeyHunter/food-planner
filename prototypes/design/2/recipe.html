Crispy salmon rice bowl

Feeds 4scales every amount
4

Ingredients

  • 600 gSalmon filletskin on, cut in 4
  • 300 gSushi rice
  • 2 pcsAvocadosliced
  • 1 pcsCucumberthin ribbons
  • 2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 1 tbspRice vinegar
  • 1 tspSesame seeds

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice, then simmer it covered for 12 minutes. Stir in the vinegar.
  2. Pat the salmon dry and fry skin side down on medium-high for 5 minutes until crisp.
  3. Flip, add the soy sauce, and cook 2 more minutes.
  4. Build the bowls: rice, salmon, avocado, cucumber. Finish with sesame.
