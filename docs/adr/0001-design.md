# 0001 Design: Fresh market

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: accepted. Decided by the manager agent (the user delegated the pick overnight).

Five designers each drew Browse, Recipe and Week with the good-css skill (prototypes/design/1-5). Comparison page: https://claude.ai/artifact/XS9cChkZBhX3KN3e8adXLg

**Picked: 1, Fresh market.** Crisp off-white, leafy green primary, warm amber for Runi Lee and the playful notes, an illustrated dish card.

Why:
- It best fits "light, modern, slightly playful". The playful parts (hand-written swipe hint, illustrated plates) stay small.
- The week screen scans best. Adult and Runi Lee rules sit side by side at the top, with a clear met or not-met state, and Runi Lee's meal is a soft band under each dinner.
- The recipe page puts the stepper and the "Written for 4 people" line together, which makes the scaling idea obvious.

Not picked: 3 (Modern cookbook) was a close second but heavier, and cream plus serif plus tomato red is a common default look. 4 (Sticker pop) is too loud for daily use. 5 (Nordic calm) is clean but the least playful. 2 (Playful pastel) has a weaker hierarchy on the week screen.

Build rule: take tokens and component shapes from prototypes/design/1/styles.css. The user may ask to swap to another design later, so keep all colors and type in tokens.
